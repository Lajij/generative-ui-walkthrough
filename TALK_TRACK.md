# Talk track: Generative UI with Tako on Vercel AI Gateway (Banksia Invest)

About 2.5 minutes. Click cues in brackets. Banksia Invest is a fictional company and every number is simulated.

---

**[Step 1: The question]**

Every investing app now has an AI assistant. And customers ask it the questions that matter most: questions about their own money.

Here is one. "How has my biggest holding done against the ASX 200 this year?" Simple question. But the answer has to be right, it has to say where the numbers came from, and it has to be current.

Banksia Invest is a fictional platform. Their assistant gets about 120,000 questions like this every month.

**[Step 2: Before]**

Here is what most assistants give back today. A wall of text. "Appears to have performed reasonably well." "Likely ahead of the market." "Probably by a moderate margin."

No source. No date. Not one number the customer can check.

So what does the customer do? Exactly what you would do. They call. At Banksia, 14% of these chats end in a phone call. That is 16,800 "explain my portfolio" calls every month.

**[Step 3: One line of code]**

So what do we solve? Tako is now a built-in tool on Vercel AI Gateway. The team adds one line to the AI code they already have. That's it.

Now the assistant can go and get real, structured data, with the source and the date attached. No new data-vendor contract. No custom integration project. Vercel runs it, through the same AI Gateway the team already uses.

**[Step 4: After]** *(click Replay if you want to show it load again)*

Same question. Watch what comes back.

Not a paragraph. A chart. Wattle Minerals up 18.4% this year, the index up 7.2%, so 11.2 points ahead. A table with the real figures. And right at the bottom: where the data came from, and when.

The customer reads that in five seconds. And they believe it.

**[Step 5: Side by side]**

Put them next to each other. Same model. Same question. One line of code apart. On the left, something to decode. On the right, something to trust.

**[Step 6: The value]**

And here is why that matters for the business. Calls drop from 14% of these chats to 5%. That is 10,800 fewer calls a month, about $1.49 million a year in contact centre cost. Answers rated helpful go from 41% to 87%. And adding a new live-data capability goes from a six-week contract and integration project to a few minutes and one line of code.

That is why developers love this. Weather, scores, stock prices come back as real UI, not a wall of text. And what used to be infrastructure homework is now one line.

That is what Vercel brings: your AI gives answers your customers can actually trust.
